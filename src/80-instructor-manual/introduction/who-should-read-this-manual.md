# Who Should Read This?

This information is intended to be a resource for people who are looking to __build__ a class that uses the Duckietown platform in some way.

In other words, this is designed for:

- __Teachers__, instructors, or professors can use this manual to build a class.

- __Teaching assistants__ (TAs) may also find the content useful.

We recommend the materials specifically for __university or college__ classes, either at the __graduate__ or __undergraduate__ level.

It is not impossible to use Duckietown at high school or lower age/skill levels, but a key consideration in that case would be to think about the [prerequisites](considerations-prerequisites).

You also need not be developing a class on _robot autonomy_ necessarily. The platform can be used as a component of a course centered on __control systems__, __computer vision__, __planning__, __autonomy__, and many other topics.
